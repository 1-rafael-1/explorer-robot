# AI camera branch on 3.3 V

The camera module used on this robot requires a 3.3 V input; its UART logic is
also 3.3 V. Earlier documentation assumed the Grove Vision AI V2's Grove input
was 5 V with an onboard 3.3 V regulator (ADR-0021, `CONTEXT.md`), but the module
used here must be fed 3.3 V, so the previously drawn 5 V branch would
over-voltage it. The branch is therefore moved to
the existing `+3.3V` rail generated by the `AMS1117-3.3` in
`eda files/explorer-robot/rp2354b.kicad_sch`. U9 (`TPS22917DBVR`, LCSC
`C2681320`) stays in place with the same reference, position and rotation; only
its `VIN` source changes. C54/C55, J8 and the UART series resistors are unchanged.

**Status:** accepted. The schematic now powers U9 `VIN` from `+3.3V`; the
expansion connector, LiDAR and panel remain on switched 5 V branches.

**Considered Options**

- **Keep the camera on 5 V and add an onboard 3.3 V regulator or level
  shifter.** Rejected: the module already expects 3.3 V, so this adds parts,
  heat and a second regulator for no benefit.
- **Replace the TPS22917 with a 3.3 V-only load switch.** Rejected: the TPS22917
  is specified from 1 V to 5.5 V, so 3.3 V is comfortably inside its range.
- **Power the camera from 5 V through a series 3.3 V LDO.** Rejected for the same
  reason as the first option; it also moves the dissipation into an extra part
  while the camera still needs 3.3 V.
- **Keep the camera branch on the TPS5430 5 V rail and accept the over-voltage.**
  Rejected: it exceeds the module's input rating.

**Decision**

- **Rail.** U9 `VIN` (pin 1) is tied to `+3.3V` by `#PWR078` at
  `(124.46, 86.36)`. U9 `VOUT` (pin 6) still feeds J8 pin 1 (`AI.CAM`), with
  C54/C55 as the local capacitors. `AICAM_PWR` on GPIO23 still drives `ON`
  (pin 3); no firmware change is required.
- **The TPS22917 remains suitable at 3.3 V.** TI's TPS22917x datasheet
  (SLVSDW8B, Rev. B) specifies:
  - `VIN` operating range 1 V to 5.5 V, so 3.3 V is fully supported.
  - `RON` = 90 mΩ typical / 110 mΩ maximum at `VIN` = 3.6 V, 25 °C, and
    160 mΩ maximum over -40 °C to +125 °C. The 1.8 V row is 120 mΩ typical /
    150 mΩ maximum, so 3.3 V sits between the two; about 95–100 mΩ typical is a
    fair estimate. At the camera's assumed 300–450 mA, the drop is roughly
    29–45 mV typical and at most 72 mV worst case, with 8–20 mW typical
    dissipation (at most 32 mW). The DBV package's 183 °C/W `θJA` gives under
    6 °C rise, so the switch is not thermally stressed.
  - `ON` thresholds: `VIH` = 1.0 V minimum and `VIL` = 0.35 V maximum, so a
    3.3 V GPIO drives it directly. Enabled `ON` leakage is ±10 nA.
  - Reverse current blocking is always on: it activates at about -0.5 A
    (typical), responds within 10 µs, releases at 25 mV, and leaks at most
    -1 µA. It is specified across the full 1–5.5 V range, so 3.3 V is fine.
  - QOD is the same 150 Ω internal resistance when disabled. The branch uses
    R20 = 100 Ω from `QOD` to `VOUT`, giving 250 Ω discharge; with C54 + C55 =
    2 µF the fall time is about 1.1 ms. There is no minimum `VIN` for QOD.
  - With `CT` open (pin 4 is a no-connect on this sheet), `tON` is 120 µs at
    3.6 V and the slew rate is 44 mV/µs. At 3.3 V the turn-on is slightly slower
    than at 5 V; no derating or restriction applies.
- **Off-state behaviour is unchanged.** Before deasserting `AICAM_PWR`, the
  camera UART is quiesced and `AICAM_TX` is tri-stated (ADR-0023). The TPS22917's
  reverse current blocking prevents the camera from back-feeding `+3.3V`, and
  QOD holds `VOUT` low through R20. RCB does not isolate the UART lines, so the
  tri-state remains required.
- **The branch now loads the AMS1117-3.3.** The `+5V` current is not reduced:
  the LDO input current is approximately the camera current plus the LDO's
  quiescent current. What changes is that the camera current is drawn through
  the 3.3 V LDO instead of directly from the switched 5 V branch, so the LDO
  dissipation rises by about `(5 V - 3.3 V) × I_camera`.

**Consequences**

- The camera sees the correct 3.3 V input, and its UART levels match the RP2350
  without level shifting.
- The TPS22917 part, footprint, reference and timing configuration do not change.
- The 5 V peak budget still includes the camera current, now as `AMS1117-3.3`
  input current, but the camera is no longer one of the switched 5 V branches.
  ADR-0019 is the owner of the 5 V budget.
- The `AMS1117-3.3` (SOT-223, 1 A) now has to dissipate the camera current. At
  450 mA and 5 V input, `PD = 1.7 V × 0.45 A = 0.77 W`; with the datasheet's
  90 °C/W SOT-223 `θJA` that is about 69 °C rise, or roughly 109 °C junction at
  40 °C ambient. Adding the existing 3.3 V loads (order 50–100 mA) pushes this
  to 0.85–0.94 W and makes the junction marginal at high ambient. The SOT-223
  is rated for 1.2 W maximum dissipation, so this is a thermal-margin problem,
  not an immediate current-rating violation.
- Follow-up: measure the camera's actual 3.3 V current; check the `AMS1117-3.3`
  copper area and junction temperature at 450 mA; if the margin is insufficient,
  add copper/heatsink area, move to a larger package, or use a separate
  regulator. Do not change the LDO as part of this rail move.
