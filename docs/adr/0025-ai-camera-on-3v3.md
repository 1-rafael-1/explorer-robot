# AI camera branch on 3.3 V

The camera module used on this robot requires a 3.3 V input; its UART logic is
also 3.3 V. Earlier documentation assumed the Grove Vision AI V2's Grove input
was 5 V with an onboard 3.3 V regulator (ADR-0021, `CONTEXT.md`), but Seeed's
Grove Vision AI Module V2 documentation specifies the Grove `VCC` pin as 3.3 V
(the bootloader-recovery wiring is explicit about it), so the previously drawn
5 V branch would over-voltage the module. The branch is therefore moved to
the existing `+3.3V` rail generated by the `AMS1117-3.3` in
`eda files/explorer-robot/rp2354b.kicad_sch`. U9 (`TPS22917DBVR`, LCSC
`C2681320`) stays in place with the same reference, position and rotation; only
its `VIN` source changes. C54/C55, J8 and the UART series resistors are unchanged.

**Status:** accepted. The schematic now powers U9 `VIN` from `+3.3V`; the
expansion connector, LiDAR and panel remain on switched 5 V branches. The
thermal design choice is closed: U2 stays, with a bounded rail current and a
copper constraint recorded below; only the bench verification remains.

**Considered Options**

- **Keep the camera on 5 V and add an onboard 3.3 V regulator or level
  shifter.** Rejected: the module already expects 3.3 V, so this adds parts,
  heat and a second regulator for no benefit.
- **Replace the TPS22917 with a 3.3 V-only load switch.** Rejected: the TPS22917
  is specified from 1 V to 5.5 V, so 3.3 V is comfortably inside its range.
- **Power the camera from 5 V through a series 3.3 V LDO.** Rejected for the same
  reason as the first option; it also moves the dissipation into an extra part
  while the camera still needs 3.3 V.
- **Keep the camera branch on the TPS5430 5 V rail and accept the over-voltage.**
  Rejected: it exceeds the module's input rating.
- **Keep U2 and design its copper for the camera load.** Accepted. The bounded
  total `+3.3V` current keeps U2's dissipation under 0.68 W, and a 3.3 V pour on
  the SOT-223 tab plus stitched ground pads gives margin inside the enclosure.
  This avoids a new converter and keeps the single-5 V-buck topology.
- **Larger LDO package, a second 3.3 V LDO, or a 3.3 V buck.** Deferred. At the
  bounded load none of them is needed. A second LDO would not reduce total
  dissipation; a 3.3 V buck would, but it adds a converter and conflicts with
  the single-buck direction. Revisit only if the bench measurement breaks the
  bound below.

**Decision**

- **Rail.** U9 `VIN` (pin 1) is tied to `+3.3V` by `#PWR078` at
  `(124.46, 86.36)`. U9 `VOUT` (pin 6) still feeds J8 pin 1 (`AI.CAM`), with
  C54/C55 as the local capacitors. `AICAM_PWR` on GPIO23 still drives `ON`
  (pin 3); no firmware change is required.
- **The TPS22917 remains suitable at 3.3 V.** TI's TPS22917x datasheet
  (SLVSDW8B, Rev. B) specifies:
  - `VIN` operating range 1 V to 5.5 V, so 3.3 V is fully supported.
  - `RON` = 90 mΩ typical / 110 mΩ maximum at `VIN` = 3.6 V, 25 °C, and
    160 mΩ maximum over -40 °C to +125 °C. The 1.8 V row is 120 mΩ typical /
    150 mΩ maximum, so 3.3 V sits between the two; about 95–100 mΩ typical is a
    fair estimate. At the camera's roughly 100–150 mA, the drop is about
    10–15 mV typical and at most 24 mV worst case, with 1–3 mW dissipation. The
    DBV package's 183 °C/W `θJA` gives well under 1 °C rise, so the switch is not
    thermally stressed.
  - `ON` thresholds: `VIH` = 1.0 V minimum and `VIL` = 0.35 V maximum, so a
    3.3 V GPIO drives it directly. Enabled `ON` leakage is ±10 nA.
  - Reverse current blocking is always on: it activates at about -0.5 A
    (typical), responds within 10 µs, releases at 25 mV, and leaks at most
    -1 µA. It is specified across the full 1–5.5 V range, so 3.3 V is fine.
  - QOD is the same 150 Ω internal resistance when disabled. The branch uses
    R20 = 100 Ω from `QOD` to `VOUT`, giving 250 Ω discharge; with C54 + C55 =
    2 µF the fall time is about 1.1 ms. There is no minimum `VIN` for QOD.
  - With `CT` open (pin 4 is a no-connect on this sheet), `tON` is 120 µs at
    3.6 V and the slew rate is 44 mV/µs. At 3.3 V the turn-on is slightly slower
    than at 5 V; no derating or restriction applies.
- **Off-state behaviour is unchanged.** Before deasserting `AICAM_PWR`, the
  camera UART is quiesced and `AICAM_TX` is tri-stated (ADR-0023). The TPS22917's
  reverse current blocking prevents the camera from back-feeding `+3.3V`, and
  QOD holds `VOUT` low through R20. RCB does not isolate the UART lines, so the
  tri-state remains required.
- **The branch now loads the AMS1117-3.3.** The `+5V` current is not reduced:
  the LDO input current is approximately the camera current plus the LDO's
  quiescent current. What changes is that the camera current is drawn through
  the 3.3 V LDO instead of directly from the switched 5 V branch, so the LDO
  dissipation rises by about `(5 V - 3.3 V) × I_camera`.
- **U2 stays, with a bounded load and a copper constraint.** The `+3.3V` rail is
  designed to stay below 400 mA total: the camera draws roughly 100–150 mA while
  inferring, and the existing 3.3 V loads (RP2354B `VREG_VIN` and I/O, the IMU's
  1.8 V LDO, the level shifter and the motor-driver logic) are tens of
  milliamps. At 400 mA, `PD = 1.7 V × 0.40 A = 0.68 W`; a continuous top-layer
  3.3 V pour on the tab plus the GND pad stitched into the ground pour targets
  roughly 55–70 °C/W, giving a 37–48 °C rise. With the motors outside the
  enclosure and a 40–50 °C internal ambient, the junction stays roughly
  77–98 °C, below the 125 °C limit. Camera inference is intermittent (seconds at
  a time), so the average dissipation is lower still.
- **The tab is `VOUT` (3.3 V), so its thermal pour is a 3.3 V pour, not ground.**
  Do not via the tab to the ground plane: that is a short. If the bench test
  needs more area, add a 3.3 V island on the ground layer under the tab and via
  to that island. Use solid (not thermal-relieved) connections on the tab and the
  GND pad, and keep U2 off the PLA/PETG wall. Vent holes top and bottom are
  enough; no forced air is required.

**Consequences**

- The camera sees the correct 3.3 V input, and its UART levels match the RP2350
  without level shifting.
- The TPS22917 part, footprint, reference and timing configuration do not change.
- The 5 V peak budget still includes the camera current, now as `AMS1117-3.3`
  input current, but the camera is no longer one of the switched 5 V branches.
  ADR-0019 is the owner of the 5 V budget.
- The `AMS1117-3.3` (SOT-223, 1 A) now supplies the camera. Its dissipation is
  set by the rail current, not by the camera's old 5 V figure: `PD = 1.7 V ×
  I_rail`. The rail is bounded at 400 mA (0.68 W), so this is a thermal-margin
  question rather than a current-rating one. The 90 °C/W figure is a
  minimum-copper number; the layout's 3.3 V pour and stitched ground pads are
  what buy the margin. The "1.2 W" often quoted for this package is the
  25 °C-ambient capability implied by that θJA, not a separate datasheet rating,
  so size against the enclosure ambient rather than against 1.2 W.
- Follow-up (verification, not a design choice): bench-test the camera at
  sustained inference and measure total `+3.3V` current and the U2 tab
  temperature at room ambient. If the tab stays below about 90 °C with the
  enclosure at its expected internal ambient, the design is closed. If it does
  not, add the 3.3 V island on the ground layer first, then consider a larger
  package; a separate regulator is the last resort. Do not change the LDO as
  part of this rail move.
